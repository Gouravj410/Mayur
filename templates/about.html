About AutoCareHub

Comprehensive Digital Garage Management

AutoCareHub is a comprehensive web-based Car Services, Repair and Management System designed to bridge the communication gap between vehicle owners and workshop service advisors.

Our platform digitizes traditional paper-based garage job cards into real-time digital records. From scheduling a service slot to monitoring mechanic diagnostics, spare parts replacement, and automated itemized invoices, every step is transparent and accountable.

100% Genuine OEM Spares
Automated Itemized Bills
Permanent Vehicle History
Real-Time Status Tracking

TY B.Sc. Computer Science Mini-Project

Developed as a practical demonstration of Relational Database Management Systems (RDBMS) paired with Python Flask and modern responsive web interfaces.

  • Architecture: Python 3 / Flask MVC Pattern
  • Database: Relational MySQL with Foreign Key Constraints
  • Security: Werkzeug Password Hashing & Role-Based Sessions
  • UI Design: Bootstrap 5 & Automotive Design System
